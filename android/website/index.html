Open Cookie

Break the cookie. Get a daily message on Solana.

A minimal Android dApp for Solana Mobile. Tap Break Cookie, approve the transaction in your wallet, and read a short thought, tip, or small task for the day.

Not a horoscope or a prediction — just a fortune cookie moment on-chain.

Coming soon to Solana dApp Store

The app is in active development and not published yet. This page exists for wallet identity and project info.

How it works

1

Connect wallet

Open Cookie uses Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter. Your keys stay in Phantom, Solflare, or another wallet app you trust.

2

Break Cookie

Each break sends a break_cookie transaction to the Open Cookie program. You review and approve every transfer and fee in your wallet.

3

Read your message

The program returns a message index on-chain. The app maps it to one of 1000 short English messages stored locally on your device.

Daily limit and optional program fee are set on-chain in program config. Network fees always apply. Limits reset per calendar day.

What to expect

Non-custodial

We never ask for your seed phrase. Open Cookie only builds transactions and sends them to your wallet for signature.

No account sign-up

Your wallet address is enough. No email, username, or password inside the app.

Public on-chain activity

Breaks are normal Solana transactions. Your wallet address and on-chain profile data are visible on public explorers.
