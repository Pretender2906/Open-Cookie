How it works

Connect · Tap · Open · Discover

The flow is intentionally simple. Each step keeps the cookie, the gesture, and the message at the center

Connect

Link your Solana wallet

OpenCookie uses Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter. On Seeker, connection goes through the built-in Seed Vault wallet — your keys never leave the device

Tap

Tap the cookie

Tap to break the cookie. On your first visit, the app also prepares your on-chain profile in the same transaction, so setup and your first opening happen together

Open

Approve and reveal

Review the transaction in your wallet and sign. Once it confirms on Solana, the cookie opens and your message appears in the app

Discover

Keep opening cookies

Return throughout the day to open more cookies, up to the daily limit set by the on-chain program. Your open count and stats are stored in your profile

On-chain experience

Your interaction happens on Solana

OpenCookie uses a Solana program to record cookie breaks. The blockchain is part of the product — not decoration

Every break is a transaction

When you open a cookie, the app sends a break_cookie instruction to the OpenCookie program. Your wallet approves it like any other Solana transaction.

Your profile tracks activity

Each wallet has an on-chain profile that stores how many cookies you have opened in total and how many you have opened today.

Non-custodial by design

OpenCookie never asks for your seed phrase. The app prepares transactions and sends them to your wallet for review and signature. We do not hold your funds

Your OpenCookie profile

Created on your first interaction

The first time you break a cookie, OpenCookie initializes an on-chain profile account for your wallet

One-time account rent

Creating the profile requires a small SOL deposit for Solana account rent. This is standard network storage cost — not an additional OpenCookie service fee.

Combined into one step

On your first cookie, profile creation and the cookie break are bundled into a single transaction, so you only approve once.

Rent may be recoverable

You can close your profile from the app. When the account is closed, the rent deposit is returned to your wallet, subject to Solana network rules

Messages

Short prompts, not predictions

Each cookie reveals a brief thought, reflection, or gentle nudge — meant to make the moment enjoyable, not to tell the future

When you break a cookie, the on-chain program returns a message index. The Android app maps that index to one of 1,000 short English messages bundled inside the application.

The message text is not generated on-chain and is not personalized server-side. It comes from the local message collection shipped with the app

Not fortune-telling

Messages are prompts for reflection or small actions — not horoscopes, predictions, or professional advice.

Index on-chain, text on-device

Solana records which message index you received. The readable text is resolved locally from the app bundle
