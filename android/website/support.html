Common questions

Where can I download Open Cookie?

Not in the Solana dApp Store yet. We are preparing the release. Install links will appear on the homepage when the app is published.

What happens when I break a cookie?

You approve a Solana transaction that calls the Open Cookie program. The program updates your on-chain profile, may collect a small configured fee to the program treasury, and returns a message index. The readable text comes from messages bundled inside the Android app.

How many breaks per day?

The on-chain program enforces a daily limit (configured in program settings). When the limit is reached, you need to wait until the next day.

Does Open Cookie hold my funds?

No. It is non-custodial. Private keys remain in your wallet. You approve each transaction yourself through Mobile Wallet Adapter.
