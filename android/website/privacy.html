Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 21, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Open Cookie does not require users to provide personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers. The application does not intentionally collect personal information from users. However, technical information such as IP addresses may be processed by third-party service providers necessary for the operation of the application, including wallet providers, RPC providers, and network infrastructure services.

2. Blockchain Data

Blockchain transactions are public by nature. Any wallet address and transaction submitted to the Solana blockchain may be publicly visible through blockchain explorers and other third-party services. By using Open Cookie, you acknowledge that your interactions with the Solana network are transparent and public.

3. Non-Custodial Design

Open Cookie is a non-custodial application. This means:

  • We do not store or have access to your private keys.
  • We never ask for, store, or receive access to your seed phrase (recovery phrase).
  • All digital assets remain in your control within your chosen wallet application.

4. Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter

Open Cookie utilizes the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA) protocol to connect with your preferred mobile wallet. This protocol ensures that your private keys and signing processes remain within your trusted wallet application. Open Cookie only requests permission to submit transaction payloads to your wallet for review and signature.

5. Third-Party Services

Open Cookie relies on third-party infrastructure including wallet applications, RPC providers, and the Solana blockchain network. These services operate independently and may have their own privacy policies. While Open Cookie is designed to operate securely, no software or blockchain network can guarantee absolute security. We recommend reviewing the privacy practices of any third-party services you interact with through our application.

6. Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage users to periodically review this page for the latest information on our privacy practices.
